Gutterbones 1977 — Animation & Illustration Master Plan (V3)


PURPOSE

This document defines the complete sensory system of Gutterbones 1977:

Motion (animation + interaction)
Material (paper, felt, light, noise)
Illustration (grindhouse portraits + panels)

The goal is to deliver a cohesive experience where:
Every element feels like it comes from the same physical, dirty, human-made world.


CORE CONSTITUTION (MANDATORY)

2.1 Grimy, not glossy

No modern UI polish
Movement is imperfect, jittered, step-based

2.2 Hand-made, not generated

Ink, stamps, scratches, halftone
Every surface feels handled

2.3 Sodium-lit saturation

Fixed palette only
No palette expansion

2.4 Paperwork is sacred

Paper = real world
Effects = ink, fold, stamp

2.5 Every action has a beat

Anticipation → Impact → Settle

2.6 Restraint above all

Not everything moves
Silence is intentional

2.7 Illustration restraint

Never compete with dice or scoring
Panels interrupt, not persist
